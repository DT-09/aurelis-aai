Quickstart

Open the benchmark, inspect a decision, then connect the engine through the API.

Decision model

Assurance states

ACTIVE · RESTRICTED · SUSPENDED · QUARANTINED · REVOKED · REQUIRES_REASSESSMENT.

curl -X POST https://YOUR_AAI_HOST/api/assurance/evaluate \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"system":{"system_id":"agent-01"},"metrics":{"reliability":98}}'
