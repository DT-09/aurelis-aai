THE PROBLEM

AI systems change faster than assurance processes.

Prompts change. Tools gain permissions. Models switch. Retrieval and memory are modified. Approval boundaries move. Traditional review often treats these as isolated engineering changes, leaving teams to rediscover which controls need to be revalidated.

AURELIS treats the change as the unit of assurance.
