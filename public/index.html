WHY THIS MATTERS

AI changes are becoming production changes.

The risk is not only whether a model is accurate. A new model, agent, tool, prompt, policy or dependency can change what the system can access, decide or execute.

01

Capability drift

A model or agent update can materially change behavior without changing the surrounding application.

02

Authority expansion

New tools and permissions can turn an informational workflow into an action-capable one.

03

Unprovable releases

Teams can approve a change yet struggle later to reconstruct what was evaluated, by which policy, and why.

HOW IT WORKS

Turn an AI change into an explicit release decision.

AURELIS treats the change itself as the unit of assurance: identify it, understand its impact, apply policy, evaluate controls, then preserve the resulting decision.
